Rules

    No AI, bots or EAs. Every trade must be placed by you. We review trading patterns, and accounts that trade automatically are removed and lose any prize.

    You can enter more than one competition at the same time. For example, you can enter the Daily and the Weekly together.

    Who pays the prize, and how

    Entry is free. It never costs coins or money, and nothing you can buy affects it.

    Prizes in this competition are cash prizes paid by the competition partner named in the competition terms, not by Spike & Crush. When a run opens, its terms name the partner, the prize amounts, and when and how the partner pays.

    To receive a prize, a winner verifies their identity with the partner and completes the partner's payout form. Spike & Crush never asks for bank or card details.

    Coins won in game modes are separate: they have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn. See the Terms.
