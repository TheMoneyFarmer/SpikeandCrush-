Responsible Gaming

Last updated: 2026-09-02

Spike & Crush is built to be intense, competitive, and a little sarcastic about it - but it's still a game. If it stops being fun, that's worth paying attention to.

Risk Rules — Built Into Every Match

Every player trades at 100:1 leverage on $10,000 of virtual capital. At a 100% margin level you get a margin call and cannot open new trades. At 50% you are stopped out: your biggest losing position is closed automatically, then the next, until the level recovers. Lose 20% within a 2-minute window and new trades pause until the next window. These rules are fixed. The capital is virtual and resets every match.

Take Breaks

Matches run 3 to 20 minutes depending on mode. It's easy to queue "just one more" indefinitely. If you notice you've played several matches back to back chasing a loss back, that's a good moment to stop for the day rather than push through.

This Should Be Fun, Not Stressful

Sabotage cards, tight timers, and a rating system are all designed to make matches feel high-stakes - that's the appeal. But if playing starts to feel more stressful than enjoyable, or you find yourself spending more on coins than you're comfortable with, step back. No rating, streak, or tier is worth your wellbeing.

Coins Have No Cash Value

Coins earned or won in Spike & Crush cannot be withdrawn or converted back to real money - this includes every match, tournament and competition prize (see our Terms of Service). Coins have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn or exchanged for money. Play with that in mind - a coin purchase is closer to buying additional playtime than an investment.
