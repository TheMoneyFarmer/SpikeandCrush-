The short version

We only use what the game needs to work. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. If we ever turn on optional analytics, we'll ask you first and you can say no.

Cookies

  • sc_invite - remembers a friend's invite link for up to 30 days, so their reward can be paid when you sign up.
  • ref_code - remembers a partner link for up to 30 days, so the partner gets credit if you sign up.
  • Cloudflare - our security and hosting network can set short-lived cookies to keep the site safe, for example to tell people from bots.

Storage in your browser

These stay on your device and are not sent to anyone else:

  • sc_token, tw_token, tw_player - keep you logged in.
  • sc_theme - Dark or Light mode.
  • tw_audio_settings - your sound settings.
  • sc_tutorial_done, sc_battlepass_tutorial_done, tw_dismissed_announcements, tw_friends_panel_collapsed, tw_redirect_after_login, sc_terms_seen - remember small things so you don't see them twice.
  • sc_consent - your cookie choice, for 6 months.

Visitor counts

Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page visits without cookies and without identifying you.

Your choice

Everything above is essential to running the game, except any optional analytics, which only run if you accept them. You can clear cookies and storage in your browser at any time, which also logs you out.
