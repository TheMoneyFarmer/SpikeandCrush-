1. Data We Collect

When you create and use an account, we collect:

  • What other players see - while you're online, signed-in players can see your username, rating, avatar, online status and the country you chose, and can send you friend requests and match invites. You can change this in Settings, under Privacy.
  • Account information - username, email address, and (for email/password accounts) a securely hashed password.
  • Profile information you choose to provide - country, trading experience level, preferred instruments, and avatar image.
  • Gameplay data - match history, trades placed, sabotage cards used, War Rating, coin balance and transaction history, Battle Pass progress, and chat messages sent from the fixed in-match preset list.
  • Technical data - IP address and user agent at login and at session creation, used for session security and moderation (for example, detecting multiple accounts sharing a match).
  • Invites - if you join through a friend's invite link, a small cookie remembers it for up to 30 days and we record who invited you, so we can pay their reward.
  • Progress and in-match play - XP, level, card unlocks, win streaks, Heat score and quick missions completed, plus anonymous statistics about how much the market moved in each match (used to keep every match lively).
  • Discover - your live status (online, in a match, away), the mode you're playing, and your Discover privacy choices. Other players only see what those choices allow. We never show your location to anyone. We may use your general region - only the country you picked in Settings, never your IP address or GPS - to suggest players near you; it is never shown. Players you block, and players who block you, never see each other in Discover.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data above to: operate your account and match history; calculate War Rating, tiers, and leaderboards; detect abuse such as multi-accounting or collusion; and communicate service-related information (for example, password reset emails).

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

We rely on the following third-party service providers, each of which processes a limited slice of your data as necessary to provide their part of the service:

  • Supabase - hosts our database and, for accounts using Google sign-in, our authentication provider.
  • Google - if you choose "Continue with Google," Google authenticates your identity and shares your name and email with us per their sign-in flow.
  • Railway - hosts the game servers.
  • Cloudflare - protects the site, delivers pages, can check that sign-ups aren't bots, and counts visits without cookies.
  • Resend - sends account emails such as verification and password reset, using your email address.
  • Anthropic - powers the AI support assistant. What you type to it is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to write a reply. We mask personal details we can detect first. Don't share passwords or sensitive information in chat.
  • Discord - only if you choose to connect your Discord account, in which case we receive your Discord account details.

Each of these providers has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data on their platforms.

4. Data Retention

We retain account and gameplay data for as long as your account is active. Match replays are kept for 90 days and read notifications for 90 days; your results, ratings and coin history stay with your account. If you request account deletion, we remove or anonymize your personal information within a reasonable period, except where we are required to retain certain records (such as transaction records) for legal or accounting purposes.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. You can update most profile information directly from Settings, and can request a full data export or account deletion by contacting us.
