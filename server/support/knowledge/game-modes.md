# Game modes and match durations

| Mode | Match length | Entry | Players | Cards | Ranked |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Quick War | 10 minutes | 10 coins | 2-6 | 3 | yes (War Rating) |
| Blitz War | 3 minutes | 5 coins | 2-6 | 3 | no |
| Grand War | 20 minutes | 25 coins | 4-8 | 5 | yes (separate Grand War rating) |
| Solo Ranked | 10 minutes | free | 1-4 | 3 | yes (separate Solo rating) |
| Async Daily Challenge | 10 minutes | 5 coins | 1-1 | 0 | no |
| Private War | 10 minutes | 1 coin for the host, free for invited players | 2-8 | 3 | no |
| Tournament War | 15 minutes | set per tournament | 2-2 | 4 | no |

- **Quick War**: the standard match.
- **Blitz War**: a short, cheap, unranked sprint.
- **Grand War**: the long mode. You choose your card deck before the match.
- **Solo Ranked**: free, against AI opponents only, with its own rating.
- **Async Daily Challenge**: everyone that day trades the same market window, alone, with no sabotage. Separate daily leaderboard.
- **Private War**: invite friends with a 6-digit room code. The host pays 1 coin when the match starts; invited players join free. Unranked.
- **Tournament War**: one-on-one bracket matches inside a tournament. The entry fee is set per tournament.
