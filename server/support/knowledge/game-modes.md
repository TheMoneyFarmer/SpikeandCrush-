# Game modes and match durations

Every mode uses the same trading rules and the same money maths (starting capital, leverage, margin, prizes). What changes is the length, the cards and how you win. The lobby's **Compare modes** button shows this table too.

| Mode | Match length | Entry | Players | Cards | How you win | Ranked |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Quick War | 10 minutes | 10 coins | 2+ (no limit) | 3 to start, up to 4 | Highest equity wins | yes (War Rating) |
| Blitz War | 3 minutes | 5 coins | 2+ (no limit) | 2 to start, up to 2 | Highest equity wins | no |
| Grand War | 20 minutes | 25 coins | 4+ (no limit) | 2 to start, up to 4 | Highest equity wins | yes (separate Grand War rating) |
| Elimination | 15 minutes | 10 coins | no limit, cuts need 4+ | 3 to start, up to 4 | Survive the cuts | no |
| Solo Ranked | 10 minutes | free | you and 3 AI traders | defense cards only (Shield, Cleanse) | Highest equity wins | yes (separate Solo rating) |
| Daily Challenge | 10 minutes | 5 coins | just you | none | Best of the day | no |
| Private War | 10 minutes | 1 coin for the host, free for invited players | 2+ (no limit) | 3 to start, up to 4 | Host decides | no |

- **Quick War**: The full game: trade, earn cards on a win streak, sabotage the leader. Best for: your everyday match. All features: 4 cards, Heat, missions, spike alerts, crown.
- **Blitz War**: A three-minute sprint on the busiest markets. Two cards, fast refills. Best for: a quick fix between other things. 2 card slots, refills fast, no heavy cards, more spike alerts.
- **Grand War**: Twenty minutes of strategy with scheduled market events and a half-time check. Best for: when you have time to plan. Start with 2 cards, events at 5, 10 and 15 minutes, Steady Hand badge.
- **Elimination**: Survive the cuts. Every 3 minutes the bottom of the table is knocked out. Best for: big lobbies and nerves of steel. Bottom 20% cut every 3 minutes; Lifeline card.
- **Solo Ranked**: Free, always open: race the ghost of your own best run and finish a mission chain. Best for: learning, practice and beating your personal best. No sabotage, ghost of your best run, 3-mission chain.
- **Daily Challenge**: One market for everyone today. Set the score, then challenge friends. Best for: one go a day and bragging rights. Same market for everyone, no sabotage, share a challenge link.
- **Private War**: Your room, your rules: invite friends with a code and choose how it plays. Best for: playing with friends. Host sets cards, deck level, starting hand, cuts and flavor.

New players: start with **Quick War** (the lobby marks it "New? Start here"). The lobby remembers the last mode you played.

User-made bracket tournaments (the Tournaments page) are separate from Elimination: they are one-on-one rounds, the entry fee is set per tournament, and they have no cuts.
