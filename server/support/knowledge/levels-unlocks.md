# Levels, ranks and card unlocks

You earn XP by playing: finishing matches, wins and podiums, qualifying wins, your best streak, missions and profit (capped per match at 250 XP and per day at 1500 XP). Matches left in the first minute and solo practice give no XP. XP raises your **level** (1 to 20); levels unlock cards. Unlocks come only from playing - coins never buy a card.

Your **rank** is your rating tier (Recruit, Trader, Broker, Analyst, Veteran, Elite, War Lord). Higher ranks start matches with extra cards and unlock cosmetics. See the Rank page and the Card collection for exactly what unlocks each card.
