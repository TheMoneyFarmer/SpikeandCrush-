# Sabotage cards and abilities

Each player is dealt sabotage cards at the start of a match (how many depends on the mode, see Game modes). Each card can be played once per match. In Grand War you choose your deck before the match.

There are 30 cards:
- **News Bomb** (information, targets an opponent): Inject a fake news headline into the target's feed for 15s.
- **Chart Ghost** (information, targets an opponent): Freeze the target's price display for 10s.
- **False Signal** (information, targets an opponent): Show a fake RSI divergence on the target's chart for 20s.
- **Smoke Screen** (information): Blur the leaderboard for all players for 30s.
- **Spread Spike** (market): Triple the spread on one instrument for 20s.
- **Volatility Surge** (market): Double price movement speed for 25s.
- **Liquidity Drain** (market): Cap max lot size at 0.1 for all players for 30s.
- **Reversal Flash** (market): Force a sharp 10 pip reversal, then snap back, over 8s.
- **Position Freeze** (opponent, targets an opponent): Target cannot open new trades for 15s.
- **Force Close** (opponent, targets an opponent): Force close the target's best-performing position at market.
- **Capital Drain** (opponent, targets an opponent): Deduct $200 from the target's capital.
- **Mirror Trade** (opponent, targets an opponent): Copy your current position onto the target's account.
- **Margin Scare** (information, targets an opponent): Flash a fake margin-call warning on the target's screen for 10s.
- **Phantom Candle** (information, targets an opponent): Inject one fake bearish candle into the target's chart for 8s.
- **Blackout** (information, targets an opponent): Blank out the target's live P&L display for 12s.
- **Static Burst** (information, targets an opponent): Cover the target's chart in visual static for 10s.
- **Decoy Order** (information, targets an opponent): Show the target a fake giant sell-wall alert for 12s.
- **Mirage** (information, targets an opponent): Plant a fake ghost position in the target's position list for 15s.
- **Intel Leak** (information, targets an opponent): Reveal the target's real P&L and open positions to you for 10s.
- **Double Spread** (market): Double the spread on every instrument for everyone except you, for 15s.
- **Time Warp** (market): Delay the price feed by 3 seconds for everyone except you, for 10s.
- **Fog of War** (market): Throw random price jitter into the feed for everyone except you, for 10s.
- **Panic Wave** (market): Force a brief synchronized reversal across every instrument, then snap back, over 8s.
- **Dead Calm** (market): Freeze price movement on every instrument for everyone except you, for 6s.
- **Lockout** (opponent, targets an opponent): Target cannot close any position for 10s.
- **Margin Call** (opponent, targets an opponent): Force-close every one of the target's open positions at market.
- **Lot Limiter** (opponent, targets an opponent): Cap the target's max lot size at 0.05 for 15s.
- **Pip Theft** (opponent, targets an opponent): Steal $150 from the target's capital straight into yours.
- **Ghost Trade** (opponent, targets an opponent): Force a small guaranteed-losing position onto the target's account.
- **Stop Snipe** (opponent, targets an opponent): Drag the target's best position's stop-loss to break-even, wiping their cushion.

Card variants (silver, gold) from the shop are cosmetic only.
