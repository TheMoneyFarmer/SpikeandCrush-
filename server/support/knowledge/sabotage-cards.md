# Sabotage cards and abilities

**Defense cards** play at once on yourself: **Shield** (for 20s attack cards cannot target you and market cards do not affect you), **Cleanse** (removes every card effect on you and shields you for 5s) and **Lifeline** (Elimination only: protects you from the next cut, once per match; unlocks at level 6). Every other card is an attack or market card.

Every mode with cards deals 5 at the start (fewer only if you have unlocked fewer) and you hold up to 5 at once. You are dealt cards at the start of a match (how many depends on the mode and your rank, never more than 5), and you **earn more by winning trades in a row**: see "Earning cards, streaks and Heat". Each card can be played once. Only cards you have unlocked are ever dealt or earned; new players start with 4 starter cards and unlock the rest by playing (see "Levels, ranks and card unlocks"). You can save a deck of up to 5 unlocked cards on the Cards page.

**How to play one:** tap the card, then pick a player from the live ranking (search, or tap Random, Leader, Above me or Below me). Defensive cards play at once on yourself. Every card changes something real: your rival's spread, fills, stop loss, take profit, trade size, how often they can trade, or their banked profit.

**Protection:** a player hit by a card is protected for 10 seconds. A player can have only one timed card effect on them at a time. Shielded and offline players can't be targeted. No card can take a player below 50% of the starting capital in one hit.

There are 33 cards:
- **Profit Tax** (epic, targets one player; Reach Level 13): The target's next winning close within 20s pays 25% of its profit to you (max 300).
- **Spread Blowout** (rare, targets one player; Reach Level 6): Triples the target's spread on every instrument for 20s.
- **Slippage Storm** (rare, targets one player; Reach Level 7): The target's next 2 fills (open or close) within 30s fill 2 spreads worse.
- **Margin Squeeze** (common, targets one player; Reach Level 5): For 30s the target can open at most half the size their free margin allows.
- **Stop-Loss Yank** (epic, targets one player; Reach Level 12): Moves the stop loss on the target's largest position halfway to the price (or places a tight one).
- **TP Snip** (common, targets one player; Reach Level 2): Removes the take profit from every one of the target's open positions.
- **Order Freeze** (common, targets one player; Reach Level 3): The target cannot add or move a stop loss or take profit for 15s.
- **Trade Limit** (common, targets one player; Reach Level 5): For 20s the target cannot open a new trade while they hold one.
- **One Way** (rare, targets one player; Reach Level 7): For 15s the target can only open trades in one direction (chosen at random).
- **Cooldown Jam** (common, targets one player; Reach Level 4): For 30s the target must wait 8s between opening trades.
- **Profit Skim** (legendary, targets one player; Reach Level 17 and Win 25 matches): Takes 15% of the target's banked profit into yours (max 250).
- **Spread Spike** (common, hits every other unshielded player; starter card): Triple the spread on one instrument for 20s.
- **Volatility Surge** (rare, hits every other unshielded player; Reach Level 6): Double price movement speed for 25s.
- **Liquidity Drain** (common, hits every other unshielded player; Reach Level 2): Cap max lot size at 0.1 for all players for 30s.
- **Reversal Flash** (rare, hits every other unshielded player; Reach Level 9): Force a sharp 10 pip reversal, then snap back, over 8s.
- **Position Freeze** (common, targets one player; starter card): Target cannot open new trades for 15s.
- **Force Close** (epic, targets one player; Reach Level 15): Force close the target's best-performing position at market.
- **Capital Drain** (epic, targets one player; Reach Level 14): Deduct 200 from the target's capital.
- **Mirror Trade** (rare, targets one player; Reach Level 10): Copy your current position onto the target's account.
- **Double Spread** (common, hits every other unshielded player; Reach Level 3): Double the spread on every instrument for everyone except you, for 15s.
- **Time Warp** (rare, hits every other unshielded player; Reach Level 8): Delay the price feed by 3 seconds for everyone except you, for 10s.
- **Fog of War** (rare, hits every other unshielded player; Reach Level 9): Throw price jitter into the feed for everyone except you, for 10s.
- **Panic Wave** (epic, hits every other unshielded player; Reach Level 12): Force a brief synchronized reversal across every instrument, then snap back, over 8s.
- **Dead Calm** (rare, hits every other unshielded player; Reach Level 8): Freeze price movement on every instrument for everyone except you, for 6s.
- **Lockout** (common, targets one player; Reach Level 4): Target cannot close any position for 10s.
- **Margin Call** (legendary, targets one player; Reach Level 18 and Win 50 matches): Force-close every one of the target's open positions at market.
- **Lot Limiter** (common, targets one player; starter card): Cap the target's max lot size at 0.05 for 15s.
- **Pip Theft** (legendary, targets one player; Reach Level 15 and Win 10 matches): Steal 150 from the target's capital straight into yours.
- **Ghost Trade** (rare, targets one player; Reach Level 11): Force a small losing position onto the target's account.
- **Stop Snipe** (rare, targets one player; Reach Level 10): Drag the target's best position's stop-loss to break-even, wiping their cushion.
- **Shield** (common, plays on yourself; starter card): For 20s attack cards cannot target you and market cards do not affect you.
- **Cleanse** (rare, plays on yourself; Play 10 matches): Removes every card effect on you and shields you for 5s.
- **Lifeline** (rare, plays on yourself; Reach Level 6): Elimination: protects you from the next cut, once per match.

Card variants (silver, gold) from the shop are cosmetic only. Coins never buy a card.
