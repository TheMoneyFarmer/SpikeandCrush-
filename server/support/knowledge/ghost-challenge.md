# Ghost challenges and rematches

On the result screen, **Challenge a friend** makes a link (valid 7 days) to the exact market you just played: the same instruments and the same seconds of price. Your friend plays it and sees your result to beat. Challenges are free, unranked and don't count as wins or losses. **Rematch** opens a private room and invites everyone from your match who is still online.
