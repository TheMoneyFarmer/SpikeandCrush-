# Contacting the team

You can reach a human by choosing **Talk to a human** in this chat at any time. A ticket is created and an agent replies here. You will also get an email.

You can also email hello@spikeandcrush.com.

Support can explain rules, look up your own account, matches and coin history, and open tickets. Only the team can change accounts, coins, bans or results.
