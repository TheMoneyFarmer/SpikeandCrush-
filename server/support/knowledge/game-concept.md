# What is Spike & Crush?

Spike & Crush is a competitive trading game. You and your opponents each get $10,000 of virtual capital and trade real historical market prices against the clock. Whoever finishes with the highest profit (P&L) wins.

Everyone also gets sabotage cards: one-time abilities you can play on opponents to disrupt their trading.

It is a game, not real trading. The capital is virtual and resets every match. The only thing you put in is a coin entry fee in some modes. Coins have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn, cashed out or exchanged for money.
