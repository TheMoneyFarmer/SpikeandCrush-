# Earning cards, streaks and Heat

Win trades in a row to earn cards. A **qualifying win** is a closed trade with a profit of at least 0.2% of your starting capital, held at least 5 seconds. Your **streak** counts qualifying wins since your last losing close; a losing close resets it to 0 but you keep the cards you hold.

Rewards: streak 1: +1 card, streak 2: +1, streak 3: +2, streak 5: every empty slot is refilled, then +1 for each further win. With a full hand an extra card becomes Heat instead. Limits: 3 cards a minute, 12 a match.

**Heat** is a separate score on the result screen: each qualifying win adds Heat times your streak multiplier (x1, x1.5, x2, x3). Heat never changes your P&L, rank, prizes or rating.

**Crown:** the match leader wears the crown. Overtaking the leader earns one card (Dethrone, at most once a minute), and hitting the leader with a card earns Heat.
