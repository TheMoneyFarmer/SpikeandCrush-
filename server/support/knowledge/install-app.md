# Install Spike & Crush on iPhone or Android

You can add Spike & Crush to your home screen and open it like an app. It opens fullscreen with no browser bars, keeps landscape during matches on Android, and starts faster. There is nothing to download from a store.

**iPhone (Safari):**
1. Tap **Share** (the square with the arrow) in the Safari bar.
2. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Tap **Add**, then open Spike & Crush from your home screen.

On iOS 16.4 or later, Chrome and other iPhone browsers can do the same from their own Share menu. On older iOS, use Safari.

**Android (Chrome or Samsung Internet):** tap **Install app** on the card in the lobby, or open the browser menu and tap **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.

The steps with pictures are in Help, under Install on your phone, and in Settings. If you don't want the reminders, tap **Don't show again** on the card.
