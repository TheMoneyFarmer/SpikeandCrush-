# Trading basics inside the game

**Stop loss and take profit.** Before a trade, pick Pips (a distance), Price (an exact level) or Drag (TP and SL lines on the chart that you drag into place; long-press a line on a phone; Buy or Sell uses them, flipped automatically for a sell). On an open trade, drag its SL/TP lines on the chart or type new levels into the position and press Set. This works in matches and competitions.

- **Buy** if you think the price will rise, **sell** if you think it will fall. Close the trade to lock in the result.
- **Lot size** sets how big a position is. Bigger lots make every price move count for more, up and down.
- **Spread** is the gap between the buy and sell price. You pay it once when you open a trade.
- **Stop loss** closes a trade automatically if price moves against you by a set amount. **Take profit** closes it automatically at your target. You can set both when opening a trade or change them while it is open.
- Prices are real historical market data replayed in the match, not live prices.
