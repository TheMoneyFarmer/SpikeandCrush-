# Private War house rules

When you host a Private War you choose the house rules before the room opens:
- **Cards**: on, or off for pure trading.
- **Deck level**: basic cards only, basic and mid, or the full deck.
- **Starting hand**: the mode default (5), or 1 to 5 cards.
- **Elimination cuts**: off, or on (the bottom 20% out every 3 minutes, with 4 or more players).
- **Card style**: Quick War, or Blitz (fast refill, no heavy cards).

Every guest sees these rules in the room and again before the start. The match length stays 10 minutes. Hosting costs 1 coin, charged only when the match starts; invited players join free. Private matches are unranked.
