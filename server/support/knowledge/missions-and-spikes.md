# Quick missions and spike alerts

**Quick missions:** each match shows two small missions above the chart (for example "Hold a trade for 60 seconds" or "Close two trades in profit"). Finishing one pays 3 coins, at most 6 a match and 30 a day. Tiny trades don't count.

**Spike alerts:** before a real big move in the market data, the strip above the chart shows "Spike incoming in 10s". It never says which way or how big, and it never covers the chart. After the move it shows the real change for a few seconds. Alerts only come from real moves in the historical data: nothing is invented.
