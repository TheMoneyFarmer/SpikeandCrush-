# Notifications

The bell at the top of the site shows your notifications, and they are saved so they are there after a reload or on another device. You get notifications for friend requests and invites, friends' wins, competitions starting, ending soon and ending, loss limit hits in competitions, shop and coin purchases, Battle Pass, coaching bookings and support replies.

Notification preferences (for example friends' wins) are in Settings.
