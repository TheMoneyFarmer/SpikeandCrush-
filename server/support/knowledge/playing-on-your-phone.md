# Playing on your phone

On a phone, a match plays **sideways (landscape)**. On Android, and in the installed app, the screen turns and stays in landscape for the whole match. On an iPhone browser, turn the phone yourself; if you hold it upright the game asks you to turn it, or you can choose **Play upright this time**.

Down the left side are five tabs:
- **Assets**: every instrument in the match with bid, ask, spread and change. A green dot means it is busy right now, gold means moving, grey means quiet. BUY and SELL on each row use your lot size.
- **Chart**: the chart, with BUY and SELL on the right. Your cards and Heat sit under the chart, never on it.
- **Play** (the gold button): the full order ticket with instrument, lot size, stop loss and take profit.
- **Trade**: your open positions (set stop loss and take profit, or close) and the live standings. Tap a player to pick a card to play on them.
- **Arena**: your cards, who to play them on, your missions and who holds the crown.

**One-click trading:** BUY and SELL on the phone fill straight away at the market price with the lot you picked. The price you get is always the server's live price. To confirm each trade first, turn on **Confirm one-click trades** in Settings, under Playing on your phone. To play upright instead, turn off **Rotate game to landscape** in the same place. You can also set a default stop loss and take profit there, and turn off the vibration on fills.
