# Partner referral links

Partner referral links look like spikeandcrush.com/r/CODE. Opening one remembers the partner for 30 days, and if you sign up in that time your new account is credited to that partner.

These links are for partners (brokers, prop firms, creators). To invite friends and earn coins, use your own invite link instead (see "Invite friends and earn coins").

For partner or business enquiries, ask for a human agent or email hello@spikeandcrush.com.
