# Elimination: cuts, Danger and Lifeline

Elimination lasts 15 minutes and costs 10 coins.

**Cuts.** At 3, 6, 9, 12 minutes the bottom 20% of the players still in are knocked out, at least one player each time. Cuts only happen if at least 4 players started, and they stop when fewer than 4 remain.

**Warnings.** 30 seconds before each cut the match strip shows "Cut in 0:30". Players in the group that would go out carry a **Danger** tag on the leaderboard.

**When you are cut.** Your open positions are closed at the cut and you can keep watching the match, but you can't trade. Your place is set by when you went out: anyone who survives longer finishes above you, and players cut in the same round are ordered by P&L.

**Winning.** The players who survive every cut are ranked by equity at the end. Prizes use the same split as every mode.

**Lifeline.** A defense card that unlocks at level 6. Play it before a cut and you are skipped at the next cut, once per match. It only works in Elimination.
