# The live market and the MARKET badge

Matches replay real market data, chosen from the busiest stretches of each instrument so the price keeps moving in every match. Only the most active instruments are offered. Every player in a match sees exactly the same prices.

If a mode ever replays the market faster than real time, a small "MARKET x2" (or similar) badge shows the real multiplier next to the clock. The clock always shows real match time. Right now every mode plays at 1x.
