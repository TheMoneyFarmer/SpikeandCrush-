# Sponsors and "presented by"

Some competitions and lobby placements are "presented by" a sponsor. Sponsors can fund competition prize pools, which are still paid in coins.

Businesses interested in sponsoring can use the Sponsor page on the site or ask for a human agent, who will pass the enquiry to the team. You can also email hello@spikeandcrush.com.
