# Friends, spectating and private matches

- **Friends:** search for a player and send a friend request. Friends see when you are online or in a match, can invite you to their lobby and can watch your matches.
- **Invites** to a lobby expire after 60 seconds if not answered.
- **Spectating:** you can watch live matches from the spectate page. Private matches can only be watched by their own players.
- **Private War:** create a room and share the 6-digit room code. The host pays 1 coin when the match starts; invited players join free.
- **Blocking:** blocked players are managed in Settings, Privacy.
