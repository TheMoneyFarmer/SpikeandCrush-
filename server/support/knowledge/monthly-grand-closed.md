# Where did the Weekly War and the Monthly Grand go?

The **Weekly War** and the **Monthly Grand** have been closed. Every player who had entered a run that was still going or about to start got their entry coins back in their balance, once, with a message and an email to say so. Your history from past runs is kept.

The **Daily War** is now the **Daily Open**, and the **Weekend Blitz** is now the **Weekend Open**. Both are free to enter with money prizes and are coming soon (see "Competitions: the Daily Open and the Weekend Open").

If you entered a Daily or Weekend run before the change, you can still finish it from your own link on the Play page (Continue). It pays coins under its old rules. Coins have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn, cashed out or exchanged for money.

If a player says their coins were not refunded, offer a human agent to check.
