# Battle Pass

The Battle Pass runs in seasons of 30 days with tiers you unlock with XP (500 XP per tier). Matches give XP and daily and weekly challenges give XP and coins.

Everyone has the free track. Premium costs $4.99 a month (Stripe subscription) and unlocks a second track with extra cosmetics and coins. It renews monthly until you cancel, and stays active across seasons while you pay.

**To cancel:** open the Battle Pass page and choose Cancel subscription. You keep Premium until the end of the period you paid for and are not charged again. You can change your mind before then with Keep my subscription. Rewards you already claimed stay yours.

Tiers cannot be bought directly.
