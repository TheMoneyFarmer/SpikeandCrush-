# Battle Pass

The Battle Pass runs in seasons of 30 days with tiers you unlock with XP (500 XP per tier). Matches give XP and daily and weekly challenges give XP and coins.

Everyone has the free track. Premium costs $4.99 a month (Stripe subscription) and unlocks a second track with extra cosmetics and coins. It renews monthly until cancelled. There is no cancel button yet, so to cancel, ask for a human agent in this chat and the team will do it. Rewards you already claimed stay yours.

Tiers cannot be bought directly.
