# Battle Pass

The Battle Pass runs in seasons of 30 days with tiers you unlock with XP (500 XP per tier). Matches give XP and daily and weekly challenges give XP and coins.

Everyone has the free track. Premium costs $4.99 a month (Stripe subscription) and unlocks a second track with extra cosmetics and coins. It renews monthly until you cancel it from the Wallet page. Rewards you already claimed stay yours.

Tiers cannot be bought directly.
